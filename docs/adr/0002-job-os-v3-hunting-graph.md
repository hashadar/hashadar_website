# Job OS v3: hunting graph rebuild

v2 Job Signal Lab centred on a JobDescription corpus and market-pulse publication. v3 rebuilds around Employer → Opportunity → Application (Opportunity absorbs JobDescription), greenfield data (no JD backfill), and a full teardown of the v2 lab surface (public pulse page, old console, pulse Lambdas/models, Canonical CV). The product is named Job OS and is entered from the labs index at authenticated `/labs/job-os` (Employers | Opportunities | Applications shell; Decision Events on detail timelines). Opportunity status is open|closed; Application status is researching|applied|interviewing|offer|accepted|rejected|withdrawn with an optional freeform tracking note (no interview sub-status enum). v3.0 ships the graph plus mandatory Decision Events (passed, application started, status changed) behind a Job OS facade with working CRUD/pursue/pass/status/Body UI; dossier and Preference Profile stay deferred. Market pulse is out of scope (personal hunting graph, not a public corpus; see #104 wontfix). Structured fields and joins are authoritative in the database; S3 holds optional freeform Body prose only (keyed from the DB when present), never as frontmatter source of truth. Decision Events are database-only rows with no Body.
